Buying or Selling Timberland

Most land agents can tell you what a property might bring. Very few can walk the woods and tell you what the timber on it is worth, what it will be worth in ten years, and what a buyer is really paying for. Casey Newton is a Registered Forester in Alabama and Mississippi, an Accredited Forester in Tennessee, and a real estate agent licensed in Alabama and Tennessee with Mossy Oak Properties Southeast Land & Wildlife, LLC.

That means a seller can price a tract knowing what the timber, soils, and wildlife habitat are actually contributing, and a buyer can make an offer knowing what they are getting.

What That Looks Like

  • Timberland, hunting, and recreational property in Alabama and Tennessee
  • Listings marketed with a forester’s read on stand type, age, product mix, and management history
  • Buyer representation with an honest look at timber condition, access, boundaries, and habitat potential
  • Large-acreage and investment timberland
