🪵

Timber Sales

Timber markets across the Southeast have been challenging and unpredictable over the past decade — and we won’t pretend otherwise. Every tract is different. The species, volume, product mix, and location all determine whether the timing is right for a sale and what that sale is likely to return.

Our job is to give you an honest assessment. If your property has what it takes for a successful timber sale today, we’ll tell you — and we’ll go get you the best price the market will offer. If the timing isn’t right, we’ll be straight with you about that too, and we’ll put a plan in place to improve the stand, build value, and position your timber for a stronger sale when the market and your property are both ready.

What we won’t do is push you into a sale that doesn’t serve your interests.

Forest Management, On One Map

AcreMark

AcreMark is the property app we built and use on the land we manage. For the timber side, it keeps the stand map, the numbers, and the plan together, so the landowner, the forester, and the logger are all looking at the same thing.

Know Your Timber

  • Stand map with species, age, and type for every stand
  • A timber summary for each stand that opens up into the full detail
  • Growth and yield for the species on each stand, for its location
  • Timber values, so you know what the woods are worth before anyone makes an offer

Plan the Work

  • Burn plans and burn units laid out on the map
  • Thinning, site prep, planting, and other management practices by stand
  • A dated action plan, so you know what comes next and when
  • Field photos and notes pinned exactly where they were taken, with the time and weather

Bring What You Have

  • Shapefiles, file geodatabases, KML/KMZ, and surveys load straight in
  • Drone photos and video tied to the ground they cover
  • Satellite and USGS imagery underneath it all

Timber tools come in the Land and Complete editions. Burn plans and the full action plan are in Complete.

Hunting & Wildlife, On One Map

AcreMark

Most hunting properties are run out of a group text and a truck console: stand locations in somebody’s head, harvest numbers in a notebook, trail camera pictures on three different phones. AcreMark puts all of it on one map that works in the woods.

Run the Hunt

  • Stands, blinds, feeders, food plots, and trail cameras pinned on a satellite map
  • Hunt sign-ups and a group board, so everyone sees who is hunting where in real time
  • Works offline where there is no signal

Track the Wildlife

  • Harvest records for deer, turkey, bobcat, and other game and non-game species
  • Trail camera photos filed by location, with the time and the weather when each was taken
  • Habitat work and food plots tied to the stands they support

Keep It Yours

  • Invite-only: the landowner decides who gets in
  • Each hunter chooses which of their own entries to share
  • The landowner can keep sections private

For Landowners Who Lease

  • Hunt lease tracking alongside the rest of the property
  • Hunters use the tools without being able to change what the landowner set up

Built around the Hunt & Wildlife Management edition, also in Complete.
